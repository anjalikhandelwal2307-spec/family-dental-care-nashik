About us

Meet Dr. Rajkumar Makhijani

Dr. Makhijani leads Family Dental Care in Nashik Road. He believes that good dentistry should be gentle, honest and easy to understand, so every patient knows what is being done and why.

He is a proud member of the Indian Dental Association, Nashik, and keeps learning new techniques, including implant training abroad, so that his patients get modern, safe care close to home.

Part of the Indian Dental Association, Nashik

Dr. Makhijani is a member of the Indian Dental Association (IDA), Nashik branch, the country's largest body of dentists. He attends its meetings and scientific events, where dentists share new methods and good practice, and brings that learning back to his patients.

Our approach

Treating every patient like family

At Family Dental Care, we treat patients as family members, not appointments. Children, parents and grandparents all come to the same clinic, and we make time for each of them.

Before any treatment, you will hear what we found, what we recommend and what it will cost. We go at your pace, check that you are comfortable, and never rush.
