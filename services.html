Our services

Dental care, explained simply

At Family Dental Care, you do not have to go from clinic to clinic. Whether you need a painful tooth taken out, a missing tooth replaced with an implant, or relief from a tight and aching jaw, our team looks after it all under one roof.

Dental words can sound scary, so below we explain every treatment in simple language: what it is, why it may be needed and what it feels like. Dr. Makhijani always examines you first, tells you the options and the cost, and starts only when you are comfortable. If you are nervous, we also offer sedation to help you relax.

Pick a treatment below to read more, or book a visit and we will guide you.

Oral Surgery Implants & Bone Grafting Specialty Services
Category 01

Oral Surgery

Safe, gentle procedures for teeth, gums and the mouth, with options to keep you calm.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth are the last teeth to arrive and often get stuck or grow crooked. We remove them gently to stop pain, swelling and infection.

Book this treatment ▸

Tooth Extractions

If a tooth is too damaged to save, taking it out relieves pain and protects the teeth next to it. The area is numbed, so you stay comfortable.

Book this treatment ▸

Canine Exposure

Sometimes a pointed corner tooth stays hidden under the gum. A small procedure uncovers it so braces can guide it into place.

Book this treatment ▸

Oral Biopsy

If a sore, lump or patch in your mouth does not heal, we take a tiny sample and test it, so you know exactly what it is.

Book this treatment ▸

IV Sedation

Medicine through a small tube in your arm helps you feel deeply relaxed during treatment. Most people remember very little afterwards.

Book this treatment ▸

General Anesthesia

For bigger surgeries, you sleep through the whole treatment and feel nothing, while our team watches you closely the entire time.

Book this treatment ▸
Category 02

Implants and Bone Grafting

Building a strong base in the jaw, so that implants last.

Bone Grafting

If the jawbone has become thin, we add bone material that your body turns into strong, natural bone. It gives an implant something firm to hold on to.

Book this treatment ▸

Ridge Augmentation

After a tooth is lost, the gum ridge can shrink. This treatment rebuilds its height and width so a new tooth fits and looks natural.

Book this treatment ▸

Sinus Lifts

The bone above your upper back teeth can be too thin for an implant. We gently lift the sinus floor and add bone to make room.

Book this treatment ▸
Category 03

Specialty Services

Replacement teeth that look natural, and relief for a tight, painful jaw.

Dental Implants

A small titanium post goes into the jaw and works like a tooth root. A crown on top looks, feels and works like a real tooth.

Book this treatment ▸

Implant Supported Dentures

Dentures that click onto a few implants stay firmly in place. No slipping when you eat, talk or laugh.

Book this treatment ▸

Single Tooth Implant

Missing just one tooth? An implant and crown fill the gap without grinding down the healthy teeth beside it.

Book this treatment ▸

Botox for TMD

Tiny Botox injections relax the jaw muscles that clench too hard, easing jaw pain, tightness and headaches.

Book this treatment ▸
